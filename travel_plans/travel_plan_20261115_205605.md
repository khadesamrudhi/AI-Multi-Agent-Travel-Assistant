### 6️⃣ Miscellaneous Tips to Stay Under Budget
1. **Book flights & hotels together on “flight‑hotel bundles”** (e.g., MakeMyTrip, Cleartrip) – you can shave off 5‑10 % off each.
2. **Use an IC card (Suica/Pasmo, Icoca, Kitaca)** – you get a small discount on subway/bus fares and avoid buying single tickets.
3. **Eat at convenience‑store “bento” counters** – quality is surprisingly good and costs only ₹ 500‑₹ 800.
4. **Free Wi‑Fi** – most train stations, cafés, and public areas have free Wi‑Fi; avoid pricey data‑roaming plans.
5. **Travel Light** – most low‑cost carriers charge extra for checked baggage. A carry‑on + personal item is enough for a 7‑day trip.
6. **Cash vs Card** – While credit cards are accepted in most hotels and restaurants, small stalls and temples prefer cash. Withdraw a reasonable amount (₹ 30,000) at the airport; you’ll get a good exchange rate.
