## 🗺️ Detailed Daily Plans (What to See, How Long, Tips)

### Day 1 – Arrival & Orientation
- **Transport:** CDG → RER B (Paris Nord) → **Saint‑Michel‑Notre‑Dame** (Zone 1). Ticket €10.30 (incl. airport surcharge).  
- **Must‑see:** **Sainte‑Chapelle** (stained‑glass marvel). Buy a combined ticket with **Notre‑Dame** to save €2.  
- **Tip:** Keep a **paper map** or offline map app (Maps.me) for the first few hours; metro signs can be confusing.

### Day 2 – Louvre & Champs‑Élysées
- **Louvre:** Book **2‑hour “Highlights”** ticket (€17) online; arrive 15 min before slot.  
- **Arc de Triomphe:** Climb (6 min walk, 284 steps) – ticket €13, open until 10 pm (summer).  
- **Evening:** **Le Perchoir** rooftop (open 5 pm‑midnight, €12 entry on weekends). Arrive early for a good spot.

### Day 3 – Musée d’Orsay & Latin Quarter
- **Musée d’Orsay:** Ticket €16; free first Sunday of each month.  
- **Shakespeare & Company:** Open 10 am‑6 pm; buy a bookmark as a souvenir.  
- **Night cruise:** **Bateaux‑Mouches** 1‑hour dinner cruise (€95). Book a window seat for the Eiffel Tower view.

### Day 4 – Montmartre & Le Marais
- **Sacré‑Cœur:** Free entry; dome access €6.  
- **Le Marais:** Check out **Merci** concept store (donation‑based café).  
- **Dinner:** **Le Mary Celeste** – try the **octopus** and **cured pork belly**.

### Day 5 – Versailles Day‑Trip
- **RER C**: Buy a **“Versailles”** zone 4 ticket (€7.10).  
- **Palace ticket:** €20 (includes gardens on days with fountain shows).  
- **Return:** Use the **RER C** back to **Invalides**, then metro to hotel.  

### Day 6 – Last‑Minute & Departure
- **Galeries Lafayette:** Free entry; rooftop terrace offers a free panoramic view (great for final photos).  
- **Transport to CDG:** RER B (direct) – 45 min, €10.30. Arrive at least 2 h before departure.
