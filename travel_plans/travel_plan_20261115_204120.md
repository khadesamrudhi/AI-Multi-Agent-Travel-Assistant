## 5️⃣ DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo**  
- **Morning:** Land at Narita (or Haneda). Clear customs, collect luggage.  
- **Transport:** Narita Express (N’EX) to **Shinjuku Station** (≈ 1 h, covered by JR Pass if activated later – otherwise $30 single ticket).  
- **Check‑in:** Shinjuku Granbell Hotel → freshen up.  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Walk to **Shibuya Crossing** – iconic scramble.  
  - Explore **Shibuya Center‑Gai** & **Hachikō Statue**.  
- **Evening:**  
  - Head up to **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** (free observation deck).  
  - Dinner in **Omoide Yokocho** (tiny izakayas) – try yakitori & sake.  
- **Night:** Optional rooftop bar at the hotel for city lights.

---

### **Day 2 – Classic Tokyo**  
- **Morning:**  
  - Take the **Ginza Line** to **Asakusa**.  
  - Visit **Senso‑ji Temple** & Nakamise Shopping Street (souvenirs, street snacks).  
- **Midday:**  
  - Hop on the **Toei Oedo Line** to **Tokyo Skytree** – ticket $20, go up to the Tembo Deck (350 m).  
  - Lunch at **Solamachi** (many ramen & tempura stalls).  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Head to **Akihabara** – electronics, anime, maid cafés.  
  - If you love pop‑culture, drop by **Mandarake** and **Super Potato** retro game shop.  
- **Evening:**  
  - Return to Shinjuku.  
  - Experience **Golden Gai** (tiny bars) or **Kabukicho** for nightlife.  

---

### **Day 3 – Hakone & Mt. Fuji Views** *(Day‑trip, overnight in Hakone)*  
- **Early Train:** JR Tokaido Shinkansen (Hikari) from **Tokyo → Odawara** (≈ 35 min, covered by JR Pass).  
- **Hakone Free Pass** (purchase at Odawara station).  
- **Activities:**  
  1. **Hakone Open‑Air Museum** – sculptures + Picasso Pavilion.  
  2. **Owakudani Valley** – volcanic steam, black “eggs” (cooked in hot springs).  
  3. **Lake Ashi** – pirate‑ship cruise with Mt. Fuji backdrop (weather permitting).  
  4. **Hakone Ropeway** – panoramic views.  
- **Evening:** Check‑in at **Hakone Gora Kadan Ryokan**.  
- **Dinner:** Traditional kaiseki (multi‑course) served in your tatami room.  
- **Relax:** Private onsen soak before bedtime.

---

### **Day 4 – Travel to Kyoto**  
- **Morning:** Breakfast at ryokan (Japanese set).  
- **Transfer:** Take the **Odawara → Kyoto** Shinkansen (Hikari, ~2 h).  
- **Check‑in:** Hotel Granvia Kyoto (directly above Kyoto Station).  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - **Fushimi Inari Taisha** – hike through 10,000 torii gates (30‑45 min walk).  
  - Snack on **inari sushi** and **yakitori** at stalls near the shrine.  
- **Evening:**  
  - Stroll through **Gion** (geisha district).  
  - Dinner at **Gion Kappa** (affordable izakaya) – try yudofu (tofu hot‑pot).  

---

### **Day 5 – Kyoto Cultural Immersion**  
- **Morning:**  
  - Visit **Kinkaku‑ji (Golden Pavilion)** – early to avoid crowds.  
  - Then head to **Ryoan‑ji** (rock garden).  
- **Lunch:** Near **Nishiki Market** – sample takoyaki, matcha sweets, and fresh sushi.  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - **Arashiyama** – Bamboo Grove, Tenryū‑ji Temple, and the **Iwatayama Monkey Park** (short hike).  
  - Optional river‑boat ride on the **Hozu River** (≈ $15).  
- **Evening:** Return to central Kyoto.  
  - Dinner at **Pontocho Alley** – choose a riverside restaurant for kaiseki or yakitori.  

---

### **Day 6 – Osaka – Food & Fun**  
- **Morning:** Shinkansen **Kyoto → Osaka** (≈ 15 min).  
- **Check‑in:** Swissôtel Nankai Osaka (right above Nankai Namba Station).  
- **Midday:**  
  - **Osaka Castle** – explore the museum and park grounds.  
  - Lunch at **Osaka Station City** – try **okonomiyaki** (savory pancake).  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Head to **Dotonbori** – neon sign “Glico Man”, street food stalls.  
  - Must‑try: **takoyaki**, **kushikatsu**, **crepe**.  
- **Evening:**  
  - **Umeda Sky Building – Floating Garden Observatory** (sunset view).  
  - Dinner at **Kushikatsu Daruma** (deep‑fried skewers).  

---

### **Day 7 – Departure**  
- **Morning:**  
  - Quick visit to **Kuromon Ichiba Market** for fresh fruit, sushi, or a last‑minute souvenir.  
  - Check‑out (store luggage at hotel if flight is later).  
- **Transport to Airport:**  
  - Take the **Nankai Airport Express** from Namba → Kansai International Airport (≈ 45 min, included with hotel’s “airport shuttle” ticket).  
- **Flight:** Depart Kansai (KIX) for home.
