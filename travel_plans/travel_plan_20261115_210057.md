## 10️⃣ Practical Tips & Checklist  

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **Currency** | Carry a small amount of cash (¥ 5 000) for street‑food; most places accept credit cards & IC cards (Suica/Pasmo, ICOCA). |
| **IC Card** | Purchase a **Suica** (Tokyo) or **ICOCA** (Osaka) prepaid card (¥ 2 000 deposit) – works on most metros, buses & convenience stores. |
| **Language** | English signage is common in airports, major stations & tourist spots. Download **Google Translate** offline pack for Japanese. |
| **Connectivity** | Your eSIM works nationwide; keep a backup pocket‑Wi‑Fi rental (≈ ₹ 1 200/day) if you need a dedicated hotspot. |
| **Packing** | December is cool‑cold: Tokyo ~10‑15 °C, Osaka ~8‑13 °C. Pack a warm jacket, layers, comfortable walking shoes, and a compact umbrella. |
| **Health & Safety** | Japan is very safe. Carry a basic first‑aid kit; pharmacies are plentiful. |
| **Cultural etiquette** | Remove shoes when entering temples/ryokans, speak softly on public transport, and always queue in line. |
| **Time‑zone** | Japan is **IST + 3 ½ hrs** – a short jet‑lag for most Indian travelers. Try to stay hydrated and get sunlight on day 2 to reset. |
