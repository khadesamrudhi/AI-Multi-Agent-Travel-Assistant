## Day‑by‑Day Itinerary  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 06:30 am – 09:30 am | Flight lands at Narita (or Haneda). Take **Keisei Skyliner** to **Ueno** (≈ 45 min). |
| 10:00 am | Check‑in at **APA Hotel Shinjuku‑Kabukicho** (store luggage if early). |
| 11:00 am – 01:00 pm | Walk around **Shinjuku Gyoen** (free entry for JR Pass holders). |
| 01:30 pm – 02:30 pm | Lunch – try a **tonkatsu** set at a nearby chain (≈ ₹800). |
| 03:00 pm – 06:00 pm | Explore **Shibuya** – crossing, Hachikō statue, **Shibuya Sky** observation deck (₹2,200). |
| 07:00 pm | Dinner in **Omoide Yokocho** (yakitori, ramen – ₹1,200). |
| 09:00 pm | Return to hotel – rest. |

### **Day 2 – Tokyo Highlights**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 08:00 am | Breakfast at hotel (included). |
| 09:00 am – 12:00 pm | **Asakusa** – Senso‑ji Temple, Nakamise‑shopping street (souvenirs, snacks). |
| 12:30 pm – 01:30 pm | Lunch – **tempura** at a modest shop (≈ ₹1,000). |
| 02:00 pm – 04:30 pm | **Ueno Park** – Tokyo National Museum (₹1,500). |
| 05:00 pm – 07:00 pm | **Akihabara** – electronics, anime stores (window‑shopping). |
| 07:30 pm | Dinner – **sushi conveyor belt** (≈ ₹1,500). |
| 09:00 pm | Optional night‑view at **Tokyo Tower** (₹1,800) or head back early. |

### **Day 3 – Day‑trip to Nikko (or Mt. Fuji)**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 07:00 am | Take JR **Tobu Nikko Line** (covered by JR Pass) – 2 hr ride. |
| 09:30 am – 12:30 pm | **Toshogu Shrine**, **Rinno‑ji**, **Futarasan Shrine** (all free). |
| 01:00 pm | Lunch – local soba (≈ ₹900). |
| 02:30 pm – 04:30 pm | Walk around **Lake Chuzenji** & **Kegon Falls** (small parking fee if you drive, otherwise free). |
| 05:30 pm | Return to Tokyo. |

## How to Keep the Budget Tight

| Tip | How it Helps |
|-----|--------------|
| **Book flights 2‑3 months early** | AirAsia round‑trip often drops to ~₹37k when you catch the “early‑bird” sale. |
| **Buy the 7‑day JR Pass** | Even with only 4 long‑distance rides (Tokyo‑Kyoto, Kyoto‑Osaka, return to Tokyo, plus local trains) the pass pays for itself vs buying individual Shinkansen tickets. |
| **Stay in business‑hotel chains** (APA, Daiwa Roynet, Hilton) | They offer free Wi‑Fi, breakfast, and are near stations, saving on transport and meals. |
| **Eat like a local** | Convenience‑store bento, ramen shops, and street‑food stalls keep lunch under ₹1,000 per person. |
| **Free or low‑cost attractions** | Temples, parks, and walking districts (Gion, Shibuya, Dotonbori) cost nothing or a nominal entry fee. |
| **Use cash‑less payment** | Many places accept **Suica / Pasmo** (Tokyo) or **ICOCA** (Kansai) – you can preload the card and avoid exchange‑rate hassles. |
| **Travel insurance** | A basic 7‑day policy for two people is ~₹1,500 and protects against unexpected medical costs. |
