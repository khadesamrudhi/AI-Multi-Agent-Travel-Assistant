Kansai → Osaka: **JR Haruka Express** (≈ ¥ 2,900) or **Nankai Airport Express** (≈ ¥ 1,500).  

### 2. Japan Rail Pass (7‑day Ordinary)  
- **Cost:** ¥ 29,650 ≈ ₹ 21,800 per adult.  
- **Why:** Covers all Shinkansen (except Nozomi), limited‑express, local JR lines, and many city‑to‑city trips.  
- **Purchase:** Buy online (JR Pass website or authorized travel agents) **before** arriving in Japan; you’ll receive an exchange voucher to swap for the actual pass at a JR office in Narita/Kansai.  

### 3. Accommodation (6 nights) – Budget‑friendly yet comfortable

| Kyoto (2 nights) | **Daiwa Roynet Hotel Kyoto Shijo Karasuma** | 7,000 – 8,000 | Walk to Nishiki Market & subway. |
| Osaka (2 nights) | **Hotel Sunroute Osaka Namba** | 7,500 – 8,500 | Steps from Dotonbori, great value. |

*If you prefer a capsule experience, **The Millennials Shibuya** (Tokyo) and **Capsule Hotel Anshin Oyado Premier** (Osaka) are ~¥ 5,000/night and include communal baths.*  

**Booking tip:** Use **Agoda**, **Booking.com**, or **Japanican** with “free‑cancellation” filter. Look for “early‑bird” discounts (often 10‑15 % off).
